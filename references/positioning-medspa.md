## NAME (MAGIC)
**The 24/7 Front Desk That Never Misses a Consultation**
(Zero missed consult calls · Avatar: med spa owners · Goal: more consultations booked · Always-on · No hiring)

## WHO IT'S FOR
Med spas, laser clinics, and medical aesthetics practices whose front desk is also
the treatment room. Small team — the person who answers the phone is often the same
person mid-facial, mid-laser, or assisting injectables. They run online booking
(Mangomint / Vagaro / Jane / Square) but the phone is the leak: it rings during a
60-minute treatment, after hours, or on closed days and no one can pick up.

## THE PROBLEM (their bleeding neck)
These clinics already have an online booking widget. That's not the gap. The gap is
the phone call from a potential client who has a question before they'll book — "Am I
a candidate for laser?" / "What's the downtime on a HydraFacial?" / "Do you take
insurance?" — a consultation-qualifying question a booking page can't answer. That
caller doesn't leave a voicemail. They go to the next clinic.

Every unanswered ring is a lost consultation — the highest-value appointment in the
funnel — and it walks out the door silently.

Three verified realities across this market:
- **Front desk = treatment room.** One person, often mid-treatment, can't break to answer.
- **Limited hours.** Many clinics are closed or "by appointment only" on Mondays, closed
  Sundays, or shut at 5–6pm. After-hours callers hit voicemail; most don't leave one.
- **Consult-led funnel.** The free consultation / skin analysis is the entry point.
  Callers have pre-consult questions that live chat and booking widgets can't answer.

## THE DREAM OUTCOME
Every call answered — whether the team is elbow-deep in a treatment or the clinic
is closed — by an AI receptionist that knows the services, answers the common
pre-consult questions, books or routes consultation appointments, and captures every
caller's name and number when it can't close. Nothing slips. No hiring. No training.

## WHAT THEY GET (the stack — already built)
1. **24/7 AI voice receptionist** — answers every call, any hour, any day. Handles
   services questions, candidacy FAQs, downtime/aftercare, pricing policy, hours,
   and booking. Warm, professional, clinic-branded.
2. **Consultation booking & routing** — books or routes consult appointments directly
   into their existing calendar (Mangomint / Vagaro / Jane / other). Complements the
   booking widget; doesn't replace it.
3. **Pre-consult FAQ coverage** — laser candidacy, skin concerns, contraindications,
   downtime, what to expect, insurance/receipt policies. The questions that stop a
   caller from booking get answered.
4. **Lead capture on every call** — when it can't close, it captures name + number +
   reason so the team can follow up the next morning.
5. **Optional business-process automation** — appointment reminders, review requests,
   post-treatment follow-up sequences, intake form automation. Discussed on a call;
   priced separately.

## VALUE EQUATION — why it's a no-brainer
- **Dream outcome (HIGH):** no lost consultations; the team delivers treatments
  uninterrupted; after-hours calls are answered.
- **Likelihood of success (HIGH):** there is a callable live demo. They can ring it
  right now and hear it before trusting it. No pitch needed; the demo is the proof.
- **Time delay (NEAR ZERO):** live in days. Zero learning curve for the team.
- **Effort/sacrifice (NEAR ZERO):** full setup handled. No tech work for the clinic.
  Slots into their existing booking system.

## GUARANTEE / MONEY MODEL
Pricing and guarantee terms are discussed on a discovery call — not in cold email.
The demo call (+1 555 010 0100) is the first step; pricing follows once they've
heard it work.
