# Personalization standards

What counts as a strong, verifiable hook (best to weakest):

1. **Specific operational detail** — a service they advertise, a gap you can
   name (e.g. "24/7 emergency line goes to voicemail after 6pm"), a recent post
   or job. Always cite the page URL.
2. **Named person** — owner/manager named on the site. Use only if confirmed.
3. **Location / market specificity** — city, service area, local angle.
4. **Tone match** — mirror how they write (blue-collar direct vs. polished).

Weak / banned:
- Generic flattery ("love what you're doing").
- Anything not on their own site.
- Inferred facts presented as known. If unconfirmed → `[[MISSING: ...]]`.

A draft with zero specific, sourced hooks is not ready. Better to flag the gap
than to ship a generic email.
