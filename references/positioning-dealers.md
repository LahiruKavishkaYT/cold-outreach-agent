## NAME (MAGIC)
**The Salesperson Who Works While Your Lot Is Closed**
(Every after-hours lead answered · Avatar: independent/used dealers · Goal: more appointments & out-of-state deals · Always-on · No BDC hire)

## WHO IT'S FOR
Independent and franchise used-car dealers — especially lots selling specialty,
luxury, or performance inventory that attracts out-of-state and late-night
shoppers. The buyer researches at 11pm from another time zone; the showroom
answers at 9am local. Web leads sit unanswered overnight; calls after close hit
voicemail; financing questions ("will 20 lenders really work with my credit?")
go unasked and the buyer moves to the next listing.

## THE PROBLEM (their bleeding neck)
Online inventory works 24/7; the response to it doesn't. Three verifiable
realities (confirm per-lead from their own site — never assert unverified):
- **After-hours gap.** Posted hours end 6–7pm; enthusiast and out-of-state
  browsing happens outside them.
- **Speed-to-lead decides the deal.** The first lot that answers the specific
  question about the specific car usually gets the appointment.
- **Out-of-state friction.** Dealers advertising nationwide shipping/out-of-state
  transactions have buyers in other time zones by definition.

## THE DREAM OUTCOME
Every web message and after-hours call answered instantly — the specific car,
the trade-in question, the financing basics — buyer qualified, appointment
booked or callback captured. Nothing waits until morning. No BDC salary.

## WHAT THEY GET (the stack — already built)
1. **24/7 AI agent for calls & web messages** — answers on the actual inventory,
   handles availability/financing-policy/hours/trade-in FAQs, dealer-branded.
2. **Lead qualification & appointment booking** — books test drives or callbacks
   into their existing process; flags hot out-of-state buyers for the morning.
3. **After-hours capture** — every missed-hour caller leaves with their question
   answered and their contact captured, not a voicemail beep.
4. **Optional automation add-ons** — follow-up sequences, review requests,
   inventory-alert responses. Discovery-call scope; priced separately.

## VALUE EQUATION
- **Dream outcome (HIGH):** appointments from hours they're currently dark.
- **Likelihood (HIGH):** a live callable demo — Aria — they can dial and grill
  before a single dollar or meeting. The demo is the pitch.
- **Time delay (NEAR ZERO):** live in days on their real inventory.
- **Effort (NEAR ZERO):** setup fully handled; nothing for their team to learn.

## PROOF DISCIPLINE (hard rules)
- Aria is a **demo**, never a client. Say "you can call a live demo" — never
  "my dealer clients". There are no dealer client case studies yet.
- Permissible client proof (adjacent, use sparingly and honestly): "I built the
  lead-response infrastructure for a US real-estate investment firm ([[your adjacent client]])" — lead-handling credibility, not a dealer claim.
- No invented stats ("dealers lose 30% of leads..."). Site-observable facts only.
